# Paradis EZ Utilities

### Simple tools for simple jobs.

Paradis EZ Utilities is a collection of lightweight Windows applications designed to do one specific job without unnecessary complexity.

No subscriptions.  
No accounts.  
No ads.  
No cloud processing unless a tool specifically requires it.

Just download the utility you need, open it, and get the job done.

---

## Utilities

### [Paradis Capture](https://github.com/Paradis-EZ-Utilities/Paradis-Capture)
A simple, lightweight Windows screen and audio recorder.

**Use it when:** you just want to record your screen without dealing with a full streaming or production suite.

---

### [Paradis Transcript](https://github.com/Paradis-EZ-Utilities/Paradis-Transcript)
Simple Windows audio/video transcription with local processing and automatic text exports.

**Use it when:** you want a transcript without uploading your recordings to an online service.

---

### [Paradis Trim](https://github.com/Paradis-EZ-Utilities/Paradis-Trim)
A lightweight audio and video trimmer for quickly cutting media down to the part you actually want.

**Use it when:** opening a full video or audio editor feels like massive overkill.

---

## The idea

Most Paradis EZ Utilities exist because the alternatives tend to fall into one of three categories:

- expensive professional software
- ad-filled or subscription-based web tools
- powerful free software that is far more complicated than the job requires

Paradis EZ Utilities aims for the space in between:

**small, focused, understandable software for everyday tasks.**

---

## Privacy

Whenever possible, Paradis EZ Utilities processes files locally on your computer.

Your files stay yours.
